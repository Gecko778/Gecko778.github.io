习惯用语

  1. Basically, honestly, actually, Definitely, Absolutely,
  2. completely, literally
  3. You have to leave it to your discretion. 这你得自主决定 discretion n.自主决定权
  4. “They open their own shop, the shop we continued today.”

​ 一句话后面跟一个描述性的短句，用来描述细节。

​ e.g: “I used a computer, the computer that has been with me for 5 years.”

​ 动作也可以进行描述

  1. eventually 描述故事最终的结局

  2. so keen on sth.

  3. To some extent

  4. deem 认为 = reckon

    I deem/believe/think/reckon that …

  5. not to mention that

语言连贯性问题：说话没有逻辑，东一句西一句，容易偏题。

多说，主动给考官提供更多信息

准备案例

Study/Work etc.

  1. Do you work or are you a student?

    I am a senior undergraduate student at Changzhou Institute of Technology,CIT for short.

  2. What subject are you studying?

    I major in software engineering.

    I’m studying for a undergraduate degree in Changzhou Institute of Technology, specializing in the field of Software Engineering Studies.

  3. Why did you choose that subject?

    Because I think that is cool. I used to like hackers because they could easily get your account password with just a flick of a finger, and then I realized that they are all the experts in software.But now I find that this is a very difficult process.

  4. What would you like to do in the future?

    I would like to be a front-end engineer. During my four years in college, I often participated in the development of some software projects with my professors, such as web design, which are all front-end categories.

  5. What are the most popular subjects in China?

    Actually, That depends. Economic situation of China is in decline due to the covid-19. So it’s hard to say.

Personal abstract Methods (个人抽象技巧 )

假设考官是一个跟你很久没见的人，你该怎么让他更好的了解你

把中文说的更加英语化；想中文想它的一小部分

再比如，要说“我很高兴我有杰克这样一个大学同学”，很多人也会直接翻译：“I’m so glad that I have such a college classmate like Jack”. 这个说法外国人或许听得懂，但就是感觉很Chinglish。英语思维就是描述这个人是谁，描述我们过去在大学都做了什么，从而让大家悟出我们是大学同学这个事实，甚至能联想到你们当年的情形。较为地道的说法是：

He’s Jack, my former classmate. We spent some pleasant time together during college days.

因此，当你说英语的时候，不要立刻想着字对字翻译，想想能否用简单描述的方式来解释，降低语言难度，就像对一个小学生说话一样。

叙述一件事情，先把你要强调的放在前面，之后再用从句去加以描述，

  • 今天早上我坐地铁去上班的时候有个小孩给老人让座。
    A kid offered his seat to an elderly man when I traveled to work on a tube this morning. (一个小孩给老人让座，在我今天早上坐地铁上班的时候。)
  • 我原本打算在今年1月访问中国，后来不得不推迟，这使我非常失望。
    It was a keen disappointment when I had to postpone the visit which I intended to pay to China in January. (我非常失望，当我推迟这个访问的时候，我原本打算在今年1月访问中国的。)
